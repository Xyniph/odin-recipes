Apple Cobbler


Mark Pellegrini, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Description

Extremely simple but delicious dessert! You can also serve topped with vanilla ice cream.

Ingredients

  • 2 (21 ounce) cans apple pie filling
  • 1 (18.25 ounce) package cake mix
  • ¾ cup melted butter

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Alternatively, you can use the preheat and bake time from your chosen cake mix.
  2. Pour apple pie filling in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish, then evenly coat with the cake mix.
  3. Pour melted butter over cake mix layer.
  4. Bake until lightly browned and bubbling, about 1 hour.
